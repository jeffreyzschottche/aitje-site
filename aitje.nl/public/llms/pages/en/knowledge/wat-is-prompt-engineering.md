# What is prompt engineering?

Official page: https://aitje.com/en/knowledge/wat-is-prompt-engineering

> Prompt engineering is the deliberate formulation of instructions to ensure a model produces useful output. It is not about tricks, but about clarity regarding the objective, context, tone, preconditions and desired format.

### Why does the wording matter?

A model reacts strongly to how you describe a task. A vague question usually yields a vague answer. As soon as you specify your role, objective, tone, context and desired output, the result often becomes immediately more useful.

### What makes a good prompt?

Good prompts usually contain a clear task, relevant context, any constraints, examples and a desired output format. Not every prompt needs to be long, but it must be precise enough to provide direction.

### What is it not?

Prompt engineering is not a collection of magic phrases that always work. It is an iterative process of testing, refining and understanding how a model responds. In business applications, this often involves standardisation as well, so that teams can work more consistently.

### What is the business value?

Better prompts result in less rework, more consistent output and clearer use of AI within teams. This is particularly relevant when several staff members are working with the same system or when output needs to comply with internal standards.

### When do you scale this up?

As soon as an AI task becomes a recurring one, it is worth documenting, testing and refining prompts. Prompt engineering then transforms from a series of isolated experiments into a repeatable process that saves time and improves quality.
