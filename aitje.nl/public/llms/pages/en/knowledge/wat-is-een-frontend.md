# What is a front-end?

Official page: https://aitje.com/en/knowledge/wat-is-een-frontend

> The front end is the part of the software that users see and interact with directly. It encompasses screens, forms, feedback and interaction, and therefore relates to how user-friendly and intuitive a system is in practice.

### In brief

The front end is the interface of a website, portal, dashboard or app. It is the part that users interact with directly: buttons, forms, tables, navigation, notifications and other visual elements.

### Why is front-end development important?

Even if the technology behind it is robust, a solution can still be frustrating if the interface feels unclear or sluggish. Front-end development is therefore not just about appearance, but also about ease of use, clarity and trust.

### What will a user see as a result of this?

Users notice the front-end in terms of loading time, readability, error messages, the sequence of steps and how intuitive an action feels. A good front-end makes complex processes manageable. A poor front-end makes simple tasks unnecessarily difficult.

### How does the front-end work with the back-end?

The front-end collects input and displays results. The back-end processes that input and returns data or actions. Effective collaboration between the two ensures that a system not only looks good, but is also functionally sound and continues to operate reliably.

### How do you assess quality?

Look for clarity, speed, error handling, accessibility and whether the interface aligns with the actual work process. A good front-end isn’t impressive for its design, but makes sense in terms of usability.
