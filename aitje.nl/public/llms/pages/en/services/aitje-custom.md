# AITJE Custom — Bespoke AI: Everything to do with AI. Tailored to your work.

Official page: https://aitje.com/en/services/aitje-custom

> Bespoke AI: workflows using n8n or Zapier, bespoke apps, agents and 3D/VR. AITJE helps you choose and build, with local or external AI and control over data and costs.

AITJE Custom is your single point of contact for all your questions and ideas regarding AI. From a workflow in n8n or Zapier to a bespoke application for mobile, desktop or tablet, an AI agent, 3D visualisation or VR experience. We integrate models, software and systems to create a solution that suits your work.

You don’t need to have a technical plan or a suitable server. Just tell us which task is time-consuming, what you want to build, or where your current solution is falling short. We’ll help you get to grips with it, explore the best approach for your situation, and guide you through the build, testing and deployment phases. We take data, costs and independence into account right from the start.

## The approach

### Analysis & design

Start with the task that is taking up your time or the idea you want to develop. We’ll explore opportunities, data, existing tools and a feasible first step.

### Selecting the environment

Compare models, automation tools and infrastructure: locally, on a server, via external AI or a combination of these. Functionality, data, costs and management determine the choice.

### Building & integrating

We build the workflow, app, agent or visual experience and connect it to your systems. We set up knowledge, instructions, access and appropriate controls.

### Testing & optimisation

Testing functionality using real-world tasks and error scenarios, and improving the workflow, model selection, speed and costs where necessary.

### Live & feedback

Set up the solution for day-to-day use and put it live in a controlled manner, after which AITJE will process the agreed feedback and retest any adjustments.

### Delivery & handover

Together, we will check that everything is working as agreed and hand over the final product, complete with access details, documentation and instructions on how to get started.

## Frequently asked questions

### How small can I start with ‘AITJE Custom’?

You can start with four to eight hours of research into the requirements, technical feasibility and approach: €300–€600, excluding VAT. This is the research phase. You will then receive a separate quote for development, testing, going live and handover. You can continue, make adjustments or stop at any stage.

### How does AITJE Custom work?

AITJE Custom We can help with your questions and ideas regarding AI: automation, integrations, apps for mobile, desktop or tablet, AI agents and 3D/VR applications. We can also assist if you need help with existing code, workflows or prototypes. We investigate the appropriate combination of local and external AI, software and infrastructure, build and test the solution, and support the roll-out. We agree on the scope, hours and budget in advance for each phase.

### Can I also get in touch if I just have an idea or a tricky task?

Yes. You don’t need to choose a model, platform or server just yet. Tell us what you’re currently doing, what you’d like to change and what challenges you’re facing. We’ll help you define the problem clearly and choose a feasible first step. This could start with research, a small automation project or support for something you’re already using.

### Does AITJE work with n8n, Zapier, OpenAI and Claude?

These can form part of the solution, just like Make, OpenRouter, local models, Ollama, vLLM and other tools. We choose what suits your task, data, existing systems and budget. An external service has its own terms and usage costs; we make it clear which components run locally and which data is transferred externally.

### Can you also build an app, a 3D character or a VR application?

Yes, that is possible within an agreed Custom project. Examples include an internal web app for a phone or tablet, a desktop tool, an interactive product model, visual data visualisation or a digital character for explanation and training. We assess the desired functionality, devices, data and feasibility before providing a quote for development.

### I’m already coding away. Can you help?

Yes. We can review existing code and your working methods, investigate errors, improve the setup and help make your prototype usable. We can also look into rising model costs, endless debugging and missing integrations. We’ll agree first on which code and tasks to review and what budget is available.

### Can you set up local AI more cheaply and securely?

We investigate which configuration is suitable for your situation. Local model steps can run without external token costs and process data within your own environment. Hardware, electricity, server hire and management are, however, factored in. We incorporate access rights, minimal data sharing, controls and any external integrations into the design. We compare the options rather than promising a fixed saving in advance.

### Does it have to be something that AITJE has never built before?

No. ‘Custom’ can refer to a new application, but also to an existing approach with a different setup, integration or interface. The key point is that the solution is developed specifically for your work. Reusable components can shorten the process.

### When is a standard product sufficient?

If ‘Assistent’, ‘Coder’ or an existing solution already meets your needs, AITJE will discuss that option with you. For installation only or a targeted improvement, [Installation and setup](https://aitje.com/en/services/installatie-en-inrichting) and [Optimisation](https://aitje.com/en/services/optimalisatie) are available. ‘Custom’ is suitable when various components together form a specific project.

### How do I keep track of the hours and the budget?

For each phase, you agree on the scope of work, expected hours and the available budget. AITJE tracks progress and discusses what still needs to be done. Any new requirements or additional hours are agreed with you first. You can adjust the scope, continue or stop at any stage.

### Is a prototype also the end product?

A prototype can help to test an uncertain approach at an early stage. It then serves as an intermediate step towards the agreed solution. Finalisation, testing, commissioning and the incorporation of feedback are included in the subsequent phases of the project.

### How do the feedback rounds work?

You agree in advance when users will test the solution and how their feedback will be processed. This work is covered by the hourly rate and the agreed budget. Any modifications will be retested. New features or additional rounds outside the scope will first be quoted and discussed.

### Can AITJE use my existing hardware and systems?

Yes, provided they are suitable for the application. You do not need to own any hardware yourself in advance: we can arrange the necessary environment. Examples include a Mac mini, BOSGAME, Dell AI server or NVIDIA DGX Spark, your own server, a Hetzner VPS for suitable tasks, or GPU capacity at Nebius. The model, the task, the number of users and your budget determine what is suitable. We quote infrastructure costs separately.

### Does the solution need to run locally?

The task determines the environment. The project can run locally, on an in-house or rented server, via external model APIs, or as a combination of these. AITJE takes performance, privacy, costs, speed and management into account when making that choice.

### Who owns the solution?

The agreed, bespoke solution tailored to the client’s needs becomes your property, including the code, configuration and documentation to be transferred. AITJE may reuse generic components and knowledge gained. Off-the-shelf AITJE products and third-party software are subject to their own terms of use.

### Can ‘AITJE’ also fine-tune a model?

Yes, fine-tuning is possible within a Custom project if the task and the available data are suitable for this.

### What if an approach proves unfeasible?

That is why a project can start with research or a prototype. AITJE discusses the findings and possible alternatives before you invest further. Research and development hours already spent remain payable, even if you decide to stop.

### Will AITJE remain involved after completion?

This can be arranged via [Support and maintenance](https://aitje.com/en/services/ondersteuning-en-onderhoud) or as one-off follow-up assignments. Management, new models and further development are agreed separately. You are not obliged to take out an SLA.
