# Case studies

Official page: https://aitje.com/en/cases

> AI in day-to-day work: searching through internal knowledge, linking work processes and developing software. Explore the applications and approach of AITJE.

AITJE is a Dutch AI specialist in local AI, automation and smarter workflows. Proprietary AI products, consultancy, development, installation and support, with independence as our guiding principle.

- [Ten models. One team. Control and independence.](https://aitje.com/en/cases/chatgpt-of-codex-in-je-eigen-organisatie): AITJE set up a shared AI environment for a development agency, featuring Assistent, Coder and ten models to choose from. A shared token pool of €350 per month replaces nine subscriptions to €200. Model usage and token costs per task can be tracked via Asana.
- [350 clients. One central workspace.](https://aitje.com/en/cases/council-hub): Following a takeover, a marketing company’s client base grew from 150 to 350. AITJE brought together client data, tickets, projects, invoicing information and call transcripts in a central hub. Staff can search for and chat with their business experts, with access based on their role and AI integrated into an existing Mac mini.
- [25 New game levels every week. With no external token costs.](https://aitje.com/en/cases/coder-game-in-24-uur): A marketing agency took over an existing mobile puzzle game but ran into difficulties when creating new levels. AITJE analysed the game, built a reusable level design system and linked AITJE Coder to Qwen on its own server. Now, the agency 25 creates new levels every week from a single assignment in OpenCode.
- [From technical drawing to 3D product model.](https://aitje.com/en/cases/3d-productmodellen-met-ai): AITJE built a workflow for a partner specialising in contact cables that converts product data and technical drawings from WordPress into 3D models. AITJE Coder controls Blender; Three.js makes the GLB files usable on the website. Following optimisation, API costs fell from approximately €9 to €0.11–€0.18 per product.
- [Your firm’s expertise. An assistant that works with it.](https://aitje.com/en/cases/documenten-doorzoeken-en-lakken): For an estate agency, AITJE linked Realworks, email inboxes and Google Drive to its own AI assistant. Staff chat with the knowledge relevant to their role. The website chatbot uses a separate, carefully selected knowledge base from the same environment.
- [Hey Bob. An extra pair of hands in the workshop.](https://aitje.com/en/cases/spraak-naar-werkorder): AITJE, developed a voice-controlled application for a local garage that serves over a hundred customers a day. Six Android tablets connect mechanics to technical information, work orders and their administrative records – simply by asking.
- [6,900 Products enriched and translated in 7 hours](https://aitje.com/en/cases/productteksten-zonder-tokenkosten): AITJE built a workflow for a leading Dutch online shop specialising in chargers that supplements product descriptions with technical charging data, translates them for four markets and uploads them directly back into the CMS. Without any external costs per AI token.
