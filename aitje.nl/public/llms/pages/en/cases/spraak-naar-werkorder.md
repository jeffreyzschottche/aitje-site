# Hey Bob. An extra pair of hands in the workshop.

Official page: https://aitje.com/en/cases/spraak-naar-werkorder

> AITJE, developed a voice-controlled application for a local garage that serves over a hundred customers a day. Six Android tablets connect mechanics to technical information, work orders and their administrative records – simply by asking.

Case study — Automotive / Voice-controlled workshop

Standing under a car, carrying out an inspection and walking over to a computer in between: in a busy workshop, administrative tasks constantly interrupt the work. AITJE made the existing garage processes accessible via voice commands. Bob retrieves information and prepares actions whilst the mechanic carries on working.
