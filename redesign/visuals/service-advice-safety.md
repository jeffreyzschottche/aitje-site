# Eigen beelden voor advies en veilig AI-gebruik

Gegenereerd met imagegen, opgeslagen als WebP met alfa-transparantie in `aitje.nl/public/img/redesign/`. Installatie en inrichting behoudt het bestaande hardwarebeeld.

## advice-cutout.webp

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a premium photorealistic transparent-background cutout for Dutch AI brand AITJE service website. An elegant brushed brass and charcoal navigation compass resting on an open cream notebook with subtle abstract unlabeled diagrams, beside a small cream speckled egg with a softly glowing warm yellow ring. A delicate branching twig with fresh leaves suggests possible paths. No birds, no computers. The compass is the clear focal object, signifying practical guidance and analysis. Entire composition rests on a compact irregular moss-covered dark stone base. Refined organic nature meets technology style; tactile macro detail, warm natural side light, muted olive, warm cream, charcoal, restrained golden yellow. Square composition, complete subject centered with 10% transparent margin on every edge. Actual alpha transparency outside all objects, including between branches and leaves, with a subtle translucent contact shadow beneath the stone. No background scenery, no room, no white rectangle, no painted checkerboard, no text, no watermark, no frame. Sophisticated believable materials, not cartoon or glossy 3D clipart.
