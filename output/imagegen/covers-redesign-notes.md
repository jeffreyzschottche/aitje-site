## Shared design prompt

+Use case: product-mockup.
Asset type: isolated 3D software product box cover for a Dutch nature-and-technology brand, AITJE. Square 1:1 canvas with real transparent background.
Input images: Image 1 is the existing packaging to redesign, including its natural box perspective. Image 2 is the exact home hero egg reference. Image 3 is the exact AITJE brand logo.
Design direction: an elegant coherent collector's series of local AI software boxes. Cream-white matte paperboard, precise black typography, a restrained vivid golden-yellow accent, tactile photographic nature-tech collage on the lower two thirds. Modern premium editorial design rather than generic stock packaging.
Composition: one upright closed rectangular software box, front and narrow LEFT spine visible, slight three-quarter view, no top face, natural near-front camera matching reference. Entire box fits comfortably within the square canvas with modest transparent margins, no crop. Front face stays flat with straight print geometry and sharp typesetting. Top brand area approximately one fifth of the front. Big black bold modern sans-serif product title below the exact black AITJE logo. One short English subtitle. Lower artwork unique to this product and fills the face edge-to-edge. Small English footer clearly spaced, no dense technical list. Spine repeats AITJE and the product title in black. Soft realistic box shading, no outer scenery or backdrop, no large cast shadow.
Egg requirement: replace the old egg artwork with the distinctive egg from reference Image 2: ivory lightly speckled upright egg, luminous gold outline tracing the front egg shape, dark circuit-node AITJE symbol centered on the shell with warm golden edge glow. This is an embossed warm glowing logo egg, not a plain egg with a thin equatorial orbit, and not a sticker or flat gold logo. Put this exact recognizable egg prominently in the lower artwork. Its glow lights the surrounding nest/moss naturally.
Text requirements: ALL printed copy must be English except the brand AITJE, which is unchanged. Spell AITJE exactly. No Dutch words. No additional invented text, claims, pricing, badges or version numbers beyond the specified text.
Quality: photorealistic premium printed mockup, clearly readable text, crisp subjects and natural material textures, uncluttered typography, cohesive brand family. Transparent alpha outside the box.
