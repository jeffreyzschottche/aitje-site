## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: panoramic photographic website hero background for AITJE, a Dutch AI brand; an existing transparent cutout of a fresh green seedling and a small cream egg on a mossy rock will be layered in front on the right.
Primary request: cinematic Netherlands countryside at sunrise, nature subtly connected with technology, hopeful and calm rather than futuristic.
Scene/backdrop: deep green Dutch polder fields, winding canals, morning mist just above the water, elegant reeds and lush meadow plants, a single traditional Dutch windmill in the distant RIGHT third. Warm golden sun low near the right horizon, soft beams crossing the mist, cool emerald and teal shadows in foreground. Small restrained golden light traces follow the canal banks and branch into the roots and grasses like a fine fiber-optic network, organically embedded and believable. Clear layered depth: near meadow textures, distant canal, windmill silhouette, broad atmospheric sky.
Composition: wide landscape 16:9. LEFT 45 percent quiet and quite dark with soft low-contrast reeds and mist for large white typography; bright horizon and windmill concentrated on RIGHT 55 percent, but leave the lower-right area behind the future plant cutout uncluttered. Foreground grass gently frames the bottom edge, no hard panels or cut lines.
Style: premium cinematic photographic collage, natural materials and beautiful authentic rural Dutch atmosphere. Detailed grass and dew, gentle haze, subtle technology, forest green, misty blue and golden yellow palette.
Constraints: no text, logos, numbers, watermarks, people, birds, eggs or prominent seedlings (these come from the separate existing foreground). No data centers, buildings other than the single distant windmill, no futuristic towers, no HUD graphics, no neon purple, no oversized glowing portals. Opaque full background.

## Integration

Full-width background behind the existing moss, seedling and egg cutout on /visie. Replaces the small blurred landscape backdrop. Dark green overlays provide contrast for white text and a golden title accent; the mobile crop favors the sunrise.
